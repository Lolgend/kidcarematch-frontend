# KidcareMatch

KidcareMatch helps families find and compare childcare centres that fit their care needs, preferences, and journeys.

## Language

**Centre**:
A childcare service location that a family can consider for their child.
_Avoid_: School, provider when referring to a particular location

**Family profile**:
A family's child care needs, matching preferences, home location, and optional workplace location.
_Avoid_: Account when referring to family needs and preferences

**Profile completion**:
Providing the required home location, child level, citizenship, care type, enrolment month, and budget in a family profile. A workplace is also required when workplace-based matching is selected.

**Workplace**:
An optional location after the centre in a family's commute route.
_Avoid_: Office

**Commute route**:
A journey used to compare travel from home to a centre, continuing to the workplace when one is provided. It has an estimated travel time and distance.
_Avoid_: Navigation, trip plan when referring to the centre comparison

**Journey leg**:
The portion of a commute route between two consecutive stops, such as home to centre or centre to workplace.

**Travel time estimate**:
The estimated duration of travelling along a commute route or journey leg, excluding time spent dropping a child off at the centre.

**Representative weekday/weekend estimate**:
A travel time estimate for selected sample conditions on a weekday or weekend, used to compare centres. It is not an average across all days or departure times.
